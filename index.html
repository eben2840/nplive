FAQs

Answers to the most common questions about NP App.

Is the app free?

Yes. NP App is free to download. Ticket prices are set per event.

Do I need an account?

To reserve a ticket, yes. Sign up with your email, Apple or Google.

Is there an Android app?

Yes, as a beta. Follow the three steps below to install it from Google Play.

Can I reserve without the app?

Yes. Every event has a web link where you can reserve with your email.

Can I host my own event?

Yes. Choose "Register as organizer" when you create your account, then create your event in the app.
